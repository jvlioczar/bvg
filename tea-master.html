Tea Master

5.0
Vegan-Friendly Asian Dinner Chinese Lunch Gluten-Free

Tea Master is one of Brisbane's original vegetarian restaurants, in Fortitude Valley. Its extensive Taiwanese and Chinese-style menu spans bento meal sets, stir-fries, noodle soups and hot pots, built almost entirely around mock meats — from veggie roast duck to veggie chicken — alongside handmade dumplings, vegan jerky and bubble tea.
