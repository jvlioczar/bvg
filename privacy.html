Privacy Policy

Short, honest, and easy to read

Last updated: 25 September 2026

Brisbane Vegan Guide is an independent, self-funded project — see the About and Contribute pages for the full story. This page explains, in plain language, what happens with your data when you use this site.

No accounts, no sign-ups, no ads, and nothing about you is sold or shared. The only thing this site remembers is your language choice, saved in your own browser.
What's saved in your browser

This site uses your browser's own storage (called localStorage) to remember two small things, so the site feels right the next time you visit:

  • Language choice — whether you last read the site in English or Portuguese, so it opens in the same language on every page.
  • First visit — whether you've been here before, only used to skip a small welcome animation.

Neither of these identifies you, leaves your browser, or is shared with anyone. They're not cookies in the traditional sense and aren't used for tracking or advertising — you can clear them at any time through your browser's site settings, and the site will keep working normally.

Third-party services this site uses

A handful of outside services help run the site. Each of them may receive standard technical information — like your IP address — simply because that's how loading something from another server works:

  • Google Fonts — loads the typefaces used across the site. See Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Maps — powers the embedded map on the Vegan Map page. Google may set its own cookies there; see the same Google Privacy Policy.
  • CounterAPI — counts how many visits the site has had in total, shown in the footer. It does not identify individual visitors on our end.

We don't use Google Analytics, advertising pixels, or any other tracking or profiling tool on this site.

Links to other websites

This guide links out to restaurants, delivery apps, social media, and other websites we don't control. Once you click through, their own privacy policy applies — we'd encourage you to have a look if you're unsure.

Children's privacy

This site isn't directed at children, doesn't knowingly collect any information from them, and — as explained above — doesn't collect personal information from anyone at all.

Changes to this policy

If this page ever changes, we'll update the date at the top. Since the site itself barely collects anything, we don't expect this to change often.

Questions?
