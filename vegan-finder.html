100% Vegan means the entire menu is plant-based — order anything, no questions needed. Think Vegan Restaurant, Dicki's, The Green Edge, U-Tong, El Planta, Veganyumm, Vege Rama, Archie AM:PM, Doko Demo V, or Izakaya Midori.

Vegan-Friendly means the venue serves non-vegan food too, but has clearly marked vegan dishes on the menu — like Vega Cafe, Cardamom Pod, Charlie's Raw Squeeze, Tea Master, Sweet & Green, or Netherworld.

Any 100% Vegan venue removes the usual "can everyone actually order here" negotiation. Vege Rama's buffet lets everyone pick their own plate, and El Planta's shareable tacos and nachos are great for a group that wants to order a bit of everything.

Two approaches work well. Go fully vegan somewhere the food is genuinely satisfying on its own merits — GrassFed's burgers, El Planta's tacos, or Doko Demo V's pizza rarely feel like a compromise to non-vegans. Or pick a mixed-menu spot like Netherworld, Vega Cafe, Tea Master, or Cardamom Pod, where everyone just orders what they like off the same menu.

Vege Rama's buffet is ideal for picky eaters since kids can choose exactly what they want. Loving Hut and Vegan Restaurant both serve familiar comfort-food flavours (fried rice, "chicken", noodles) that go down easy with children. Dicki's relaxed, dog-friendly café is another easy, casual option.

El Planta's lively atmosphere, shareable plates, and margaritas make for an easy, low-pressure first date. Neon Ramen's modern bar-restaurant setting works well too, and on the Gold Coast, Izakaya Midori and Greenhouse Canteen both have a cosier, sit-down feel.

Vege Rama's buffet format handles groups without any fuss — everyone serves themselves. El Planta's share-plate Mexican menu is built for a table of people ordering together, and Netherworld's arcade bar gives a group something to do while they wait on food and drinks.

West End, New Farm, Fortitude Valley, and Everton Park each have multiple venues in our directory. If you're happy to drive a bit further, the Gold Coast has become a real hotspot too, with several fully vegan restaurants opening there recently.
