How you can help

Brisbane Vegan Guide is an independent, self-funded project — there's no company or investor behind it, just the time and resources of the people who built it. If this guide has ever helped you find a great meal, discover a new group, or feel a little more at home in Brisbane's vegan community, there are several ways you can help keep it going and growing.

Ways to contribute
Partnerships

We're open to partnerships with vegan and vegan-friendly businesses, organisations and brands — paid or unpaid. Whether it's a sponsored feature, an ongoing collaboration, or a one-off promotion, we'd love to hear your idea and find a format that works for both sides.

Share the project

One of the easiest ways to help is also completely free: share this guide. Send it to a friend, post it in a group chat, or share it on your socials. Every share spreads information that helps animals — and helps more people find their way to vegan food and community in Brisbane.

Suggest content or flag an error

Brisbane's vegan scene changes fast — new venues open, others close, menus evolve. If you spot outdated or incorrect information, know a venue, group or person we should add, or simply have an idea to make this guide more complete, send us a message. Every correction and suggestion helps keep this resource accurate for everyone who relies on it.

Got a digital or graphic project in mind?

Beyond running this guide, we also take on design and development work — websites, branding, illustration and more. If you have a project you'd like to bring to life, get in touch and let's talk about how we can help.

Other ways to help

You don't need to donate directly to make a difference. Hiring a vegan professional through our Hire a Vegan page supports the community directly, and donating to vegan advocacy groups, animal sanctuaries or animal-ethics research organisations helps the wider movement grow. Every bit of support counts, wherever you choose to give it.
